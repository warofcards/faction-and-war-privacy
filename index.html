Privacy Policy - Faction and War

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Faction and War ("the game") is a card-battle game published by SinglePlayerBuild ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you use to register or log in. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password in plain text.
  • Game profile and progress: your player name, chosen faction, hero level, deck, owned cards and card levels, in-game currencies (Gold, Gems, Mana), fragments, ranking points and tier, daily-reward status, and similar gameplay data. This is stored in Google Cloud Firestore so your progress is saved to your account.
  • Purchase information: when you buy Gems, Google Play processes the payment. We receive only a purchase receipt (a purchase token and product ID) so our server can verify the purchase with Google and grant your Gems. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Technical data: limited diagnostic data created by Firebase and Google Play services (for example, error logs and timestamps) needed to run the service.
  • Local settings: your sound/effects preferences and, if you choose "Remember me", your login details are stored on your device only.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data, and the game does not show third-party advertising.

How we use information

  • to create and secure your account and save your progress;
  • to run features such as matchmaking, rankings, and monthly ranking rewards (your player name, tier, and ranking points are visible to other players in the ranking list);
  • to process and verify in-app purchases and prevent fraud;
  • to fix bugs and improve the game.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by our service providers Google Firebase / Google Cloud (authentication, database, server functions) and Google Play (payments), under their own privacy policies. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated game data by emailing us at the address below; we will delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal or fraud-prevention reasons (for example, purchase records).

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the game means you accept the updated policy.
